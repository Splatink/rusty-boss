Protection that stays visible

The controls at the top tell you what the app is allowed to change. The app starts in Read-only every time and never remembers permission to write.

Read-only

Connecting in read-only still sends the editor-session setup/cleanup commands needed by the connection — it does not change tone parameters or select a channel.

Play first, save when you're ready

  1. 1
    Connect your amp by USB. In Settings, refresh the MIDI ports, choose the Katana's control port, and click Connect.
  2. 2
    Import your tones. Click Import TSL… and choose a Gen 3 .tsl file — or drop the file onto the app window. Each import adds a collection; your existing tones are kept.
  3. 3
    Choose “Preview & edit.” Click a tone in My tones to hear it on your amp. Amp knobs and effects become live after the preview is read back. Your saved channels are unchanged.
  4. 4
    Keep a favorite. Click Enable amp writes… and answer Yes. Drag a tone from My tones onto an Amp channel, then answer Yes to the overwrite confirmation.
  5. 5
    Lock it. Click Amp writes enabled · Lock when you're done saving. Saving replaces only the chosen channel's previous tone.

Saving to PANEL itself still requires an explicit PANEL destination and overwrite confirmation. No typed unlock phrases, no manual buffer-commit steps.

Run on Linux

From a source checkout, with Rust 1.99.1 or newer, a C toolchain and ALSA development packages installed:

cargo build --release --locked
./target/release/rusty-boss
  • Native Wayland as well as X11 — XWayland is not required.
  • A working graphical Linux desktop and ALSA USB-MIDI support are required.
  • Choose the MIDI control port, not an audio device; the port list is not scanned at startup.
  • Don't run the app as root or make sound devices world-writable.
